# User Communication Templates

## Acknowledgement
> Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out. I understand [one-line restatement of the issue]. I'm looking into it now and will update you by [time].

## Asking for information
> To narrow this down, could you tell me: (1) the exact error message, (2) when it last worked, (3) whether anyone else near you is affected?

## Resolution
> Hi [Name], the issue was caused by [plain-language cause]. I've [what you did]. Could you please confirm [specific check] works on your side? If it recurs, reply to this ticket.

## Escalation notice
> Hi [Name], this needs a specialist team ([team]). I've passed on everything we've tried so they won't ask you to repeat it. Expected next update: [time].

## Rules
Plain language, no blame, set expectations with a time, confirm the fix with the user.
